Who we are

Welcome to PCMS-HRG

We are the Pui Ching Middle School (Macau) Humanoid Robot Group — a student team that programmes autonomous humanoid robots and takes part in invited competitions.

Our 2026 results include fifth in the world at RoboCup, first place at the RCAP Beijing Masters, a WHRG quarter-final finish, and second and third places at GDTE in Hangzhou.
