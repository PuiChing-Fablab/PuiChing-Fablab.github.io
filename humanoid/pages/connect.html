Partner with PCMS-HRG

Humanoid robotics brings together artificial intelligence, control engineering, mechanical design, communications and materials research. Reaching a world final from a school lab in Macau takes more than enthusiasm.

We welcome support from government, academia, and companies who want to back science and technology education — equipment support, educational partnerships, engineer exchanges, joint projects and competition sponsorship. If any of that sounds like your organisation, our coaches would be glad to hear from you.
