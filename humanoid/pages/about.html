About PCMS-HRG

A student humanoid robot team from Macau — the people, the platforms and the problems we are working on.

Who We Are

PCMS-HRG is a student team at Pui Ching Middle School (Macau), founded in November 2025. We programme autonomous humanoid robots for the RoboCup Humanoid Soccer League, the World Humanoid Robot Games and RoboCup Asia-Pacific (RCAP).

Across every event — league soccer, 3v3, Humanoid OnStage and the half marathon — the robots see, decide and move without remote control. Students build the perception, motion, match strategy and multi-robot coordination that make every play possible.

Programme Lines & Robots

Two platforms, one team: Booster T1 for full-size league soccer and Booster K1 for agile multi-robot competitions.

T1

T1 Line

Full-size league soccer

The full-size track. Booster T1 is our RoboCup platform — the Humanoid Soccer League's Large Division, where we play the Major bracket against university and research laboratory teams. Everything here is about robustness at scale: gait stability on a big biped, fall recovery, and holding a strategy together across a full match.

  • PlatformBooster T1
  • Height1.18 m
  • Weight30 kg
  • Main competitionRoboCup HSL — Large Division

K1

K1 Line

Agile multi-robot play

The compact track. Booster K1 is light, quick and tough enough to take repeated falls, which makes it the right machine for 3v3 soccer, stage performance and long-distance road events. It is also where the team grows: a smaller robot means more of them, so more students get hands on a real platform at the same time.

  • Height0.95 m
  • Weight19.5 kg

History

Team Milestones

November 2025

Group founded — three Booster T1 robots delivered

First power-on, first walk, first fall.

December 2025 – January 2026

Weekly lab sessions begin

Motion tuning, vision pipeline and the first public demonstration at the affiliated primary school.

February 2026

Invited to Beijing by Booster Robotics

Systematic training on ROS 2 deployment, YOLO inference, behaviour-tree strategy and multi-robot play — the team's first work on K1 hardware.

April 2026

First official competitions

The Beijing Middle School Humanoid Soccer League, then the Beijing Yizhuang Humanoid Robot Half Marathon as the only middle school in the field.

July 2026

RoboCup 2026, Incheon — 5th in the world

The T1 line's debut in the Major bracket of the Humanoid Soccer League, Large Division.

August 2026

RCAP champions and WHRG quarter-finalists

Two K1-line results in one month, and Macau's first appearance at the World Humanoid Robot Games.

Next season

The K1 fleet grows to eight

Seven Education Edition units plus one Geek Edition — enough robots for the whole squad to train in parallel.
